ECHO MAZE

A journey through darkness.

How to Play

You carry a letter into a lightless labyrinth. There is no map and no arrow. Listen.

The signal grows louder and clearer as you near its source, and muffled when you drift away. Left or right in your headphones tells you which way to turn.

Your torch burns down as you walk. Moving closer to the sound rewards you with a little fuel. Your trail fades after a few seconds.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or the touch joystick. Esc pauses. Headphones recommended.
